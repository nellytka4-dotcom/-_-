# A pocket lexicon for a language not yet spoken

These words have no speakers. They are kept here in case a speaker turns up.

**velune** *(n.)* — the blue interval between noticing a thing and deciding what it is.

**orr** *(v.)* — to continue changing very slowly, without having chosen a destination.

**thessa** *(n.)* — a place that becomes familiar before it becomes known.

**murai** *(adj.)* — pleasantly unfinished; not broken, merely still open.

**esh** *(interj.)* — said to a small coincidence that has no obligation to mean anything.

### A sentence

*Thessa velune; orr, murai.*

Approximate gloss: *The place is becoming familiar in the blue interval; it continues, pleasantly unfinished.*

The grammar is provisional. The words may change their minds.
