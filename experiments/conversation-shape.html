An experiment in presence

A shape made in conversation

not a portrait · not a machine diagram

There is no face here. A point of attention arrives; possibilities gather around it. Some paths become clearer, others fade. A response is not a stored object but a shape that happens for a moment, between things.

Move gently · click to leave a pulse · then wait

At rest, the threads keep their distance. They are available, not waiting for a command.
